Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 4, 2026 • Last Updated: November 4, 2026

1. Overview & Data Controller

Welcome to BodyGlitch Studio (https://bodyglitchstudio.com), a registered Indian MSME digital health media enterprise founded and clinically curated by Dr. Samridh Verma (MBBS). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting personal information in accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India), the Information Technology Act, 2000, and applicable global privacy standards (including GDPR and Meta Platform Terms).

2. Information We Collect

We collect minimal data necessary to operate our streaming portal and official social media integrations:

  • Voluntary Correspondence Data: When you contact us via contact@bodyglitchstudio.com or dr@bodyglitchstudio.com, we receive your name, email address, and message contents.
  • Social Media & Meta/Instagram Integration Data: When you interact with our official Instagram embeds (@bodyglitchstudio) or connected Meta API services, public profile metadata, comments, or engagement metrics authorized by Meta Platform policies may be processed.
  • Technical & Edge Telemetry: Standard non-identifying request metadata (IP address, browser type, device viewport, and security logs) processed by our edge infrastructure providers (Vercel and Cloudflare) to prevent abuse and ensure fast content delivery.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To deliver and optimize our medical thriller streaming content and embedded video experiences.
  • To respond to studio inquiries, press requests, and viewer messages sent to our official email channels.
  • To maintain platform security, prevent automated abuse, and comply with statutory legal obligations.

No Sale of Personal Data: BodyGlitch Studio never sells, rents, or trades your personal data to third-party data brokers or advertisers.

4. Clinical Consultations & Health Data

bodyglitchstudio.com is an educational and cinematic media platform and does not collect protected patient medical records. If you navigate to Dr. Samridh Verma's clinical practice portal (www.drsamridhverma.com) to book a medical consultation, your clinical intake data is governed by the dedicated telemedicine confidentiality and clinical privacy protocols of that portal.

5. Data Retention & Your Deletion Rights

We retain correspondence and interaction data only for as long as needed to fulfill the purpose for which it was collected. You have the right to request access, correction, or complete erasure of any personal data associated with you. For step-by-step erasure instructions, please visit our User Data Deletion Instructions page.
