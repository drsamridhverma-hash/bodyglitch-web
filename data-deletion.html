User Data Deletion Instructions

Effective Date: November 4, 2026 • Guaranteed Processing SLA: Within 48 Hours

1. How to Remove Connected Instagram / Facebook Permissions

In compliance with Meta Platform Terms, if you have interacted with BodyGlitch Studio via Instagram (@bodyglitchstudio) or connected Facebook/Instagram applications, you can revoke access and trigger data removal directly from your Meta account at any time:

  1. Open your Instagram or Facebook account and navigate to Settings & Privacy → Settings.
  2. Select Apps and Websites (under Your Activity or Permissions).
  3. Locate BodyGlitch Studio in the active list and click Remove.
  4. Check the box to delete associated activity data and confirm.

3. What Data Is Purged

  • Confirmation of permanent deletion is emailed back to you once completed.
